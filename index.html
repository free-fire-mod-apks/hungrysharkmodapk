4. Core Gameplay Mechanics & Evolution System

Navigating the oceanic environment requires balancing survival mechanics, exploration, and combat maneuverability:

Control & Movement Systems

Official releases support both responsive touch controls and tilt-sensor steering. A dedicated Boost meter allows for sudden accelerations to catch fast-moving prey, breach the water's surface, or escape armed divers and mines.

Shark Progression & Statutory Stats

Every shark possesses three fundamental stats that can be upgraded using coins earned during runs:

  • Bite Force: Determines how fast a shark devours prey and allows consumption of larger creatures.
  • Speed: Increases horizontal and vertical swimming velocity.
  • Boost Capacity: Extends the duration and regeneration speed of the boost gauge.

Gold Rush Engine

Building uninterrupted prey-eating combos fills the Gold Rush bar. Once activated, every edible object turns into gold, grants temporary invulnerability, and provides infinite boost energy for high-score optimization.

Accessories & Baby Companions

Ranged accessories alter gameplay strategies. Equipping a Jetpack allows sharks to fly through the air and destroy helicopters, while Laser Goggles paralyze prey at a distance. Recruiting Baby Sharks provides passive support, aiding in prey consumption and mine detonation.
